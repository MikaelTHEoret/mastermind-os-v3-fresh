# ADR 0003: Role-specific profile depth and retention

Status: accepted.

The existing Mastermind identity and memory fabric remains the long-term authority. A local encrypted interaction journal and generated profile projection provide offline continuity without creating a second vector-memory system.

Parent profiles may be deep and may opt into Codex retrieval. Child profiles are limited to relationship and shared-play continuity. Raw interaction history remains parent-controlled and retained until deletion. Derived claims always keep evidence references and confidence; adult predictions remain hypotheses rather than facts.
